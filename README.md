Jo's Pares Mami - Defend the Cart!
A physics-based, HTML5 Canvas tower-defense game where you play as Jo, slinging hot bowls of pares and mami to fight off waves of enemies and reclaim your family's legendary food business.

📖 Storyline
Jo and his father ran the most legendary paresan in Caloocan, famous for its incredible "sabaw".

A jealous government health inspector and a clout-chasing food vlogger sabotaged them by releasing a fake viral video, causing the government to shut them down.

With his father heartbroken, Jo grabs his heavy-duty ladle, swearing to cook his way back to the top and clear his name.

🎮 Features & Gameplay
Physics-based Combat: Drag and shoot food projectiles using an intuitive catapult mechanic.

Arsenal Upgrades: Earn "Kita" (currency) to unlock and upgrade weapons like Mami, Pares, Cola, and Rice.

Act 1: The Caloocan Comeback (Levels 1-5): Defend against gangsters, cockroaches, rats, and dogs, ending in a boss fight with Inspector Kap Nino.

Act 2: The Manila Takeover (Levels 6-10): Expand to Intramuros, Quiapo, and Binondo. Battle students, workers, and squatters before facing Boss Vlogger Diwata.

Act 3: The Makati Campaign (Levels 11-15): Fight through corporate elites, politicians, and the mastermind final boss in Malacañang to pass the "National Pares Act".

📂 Project Structure
index.html: The main canvas container.

assets/: Contains all visual and audio files.

/animations: Moving frames for Jo cooking/serving.

/audio: Background music (BGM) and combat sound effects (SFX).

/backgrounds: Environment art for the 15 levels.

/sprites: Static props like carts, bowls, and jeepneys.

/ui: Menu buttons, health bars, etc.

js/: Core game logic.

main.js: Entry point that initializes the game loop.

/core/: Engine fundamentals (Game.js, InputHandler.js, AssetLoader.js, SaveManager.js).

/managers/: Game flow controllers (LevelManager.js, WaveManager.js, ShopManager.js, UIManager.js).

/entities/: In-game actors (Player.js, Enemy.js, Projectiles.js).

/utils/: Helpers and Configurations (Physics.js, Constants.js).
